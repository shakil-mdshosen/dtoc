Privacy notice

How DTOC Forms handles your data

Last updated: 26 September 2026

DTOC Forms is a volunteer-run tool hosted on Wikimedia Toolforge. It is not operated by the Wikimedia Foundation, and the Foundation's privacy policy does not cover it. Use of this tool is subject to the Wikimedia Cloud Services Terms of Use.

Who runs this tool

DTOC Forms is maintained by Shakil Hosen (User:MdsShakil). Each form is created and managed by the Wikimedia user named on the form (the form creator), together with any collaborators they add. The form creator decides what the form asks and how responses are used.

What we collect

When you log in

  • Your Wikimedia username, received from Meta-Wiki through OAuth. This is required to use the tool.
  • Your email address and whether it is confirmed, if your account has one. The tool's OAuth permission lets it read these (and your real name, which the tool does not read or store). A confirmed email is kept in your login session so that forms which collect email can use it. It is only saved permanently if you respond to a form that collects email (see below).

Your login session is kept in a signed cookie in your browser and ends when you log out or close your browser. The tool never sees your Wikimedia password and cannot edit wikis on your behalf.

When you respond to a form

  • Your answers to the form's questions.
  • Your Wikimedia username and the date and time of your response. Anonymous responses are not possible.
  • Your confirmed Wikimedia email address, but only if the form says it collects email. Such forms show a notice with the exact address before you answer. If your account has no confirmed email, you cannot respond to them.

When you create or manage a form

  • The form's content and settings, your username as creator, and the usernames of collaborators you add.
  • An audit log of management actions, such as creating, editing, closing, exporting or deleting a form, and adding or removing collaborators, with the username of the person who acted.

Stored only in your browser

  • While you fill in a form, your answers are saved as a draft in your browser's local storage so a refresh doesn't lose them. Drafts are not sent to the server until you press Submit, and are removed after you submit or press "Clear form".
  • Your light/dark theme preference.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. The tool loads no third-party scripts, fonts or images; everything is served from Toolforge.
  • The tool does not store your IP address or browser details in its database. The Toolforge infrastructure may keep standard web server logs, as described in the Cloud Services Terms of Use.

Who can see your data

  • The form creator and collaborators can see every response to their form, including usernames, times and, for forms that collect it, email addresses. They can download responses as CSV, Excel or JSON files. Once downloaded, those files are the form managers' responsibility and are no longer covered by the deletion schedule below.
  • The tool maintainer can see a list of all forms, their creators and response counts, and has technical access to the tool's database as its operator. This access is used only to run, secure and troubleshoot the tool.
  • Other respondents cannot see your answers. The list of users with access to a form is shown on the form itself.
  • Your data is not sold or shared with anyone else, except where required by law or the Cloud Services Terms of Use.

How long we keep it

  • Responses are kept while a form is open. 90 days after a form is closed, all its responses (including any collected email addresses) are permanently deleted automatically. If a form is reopened, this countdown starts again when it is next closed.
  • Form managers can delete individual responses, or a whole form with all its responses, at any time. Deletion is immediate.
  • Form definitions and the audit log are kept after responses are deleted so that form activity remains accountable.
  • Drafts in your browser stay until you submit, clear the form, or clear your browser's site data.

Your choices

  • You can choose not to respond to a form, for example if it collects your email address.
  • To have a response corrected or deleted, contact the form creator shown on the form. If they don't respond, contact the tool maintainer.
  • You can revoke the tool's access to your Wikimedia account at any time at Special:OAuthManageMyGrants on Meta-Wiki. This does not delete responses you have already submitted.

Contact

Questions about a specific form: contact its creator. Questions about the tool or this notice: leave a message on User talk:MdsShakil on Meta-Wiki.

Changes to this notice

If the tool starts collecting different data, this page will be updated before the change takes effect, and the date above will change.
